METHOD
Equal numbers of training references were sampled per class. A balanced independent test set was sampled from the remaining references.
SNP missingness, minor allele count, corrected Hudson FST ranking, and allele frequencies were recalculated from training references only.
The analysis reranked the corrected Step 02 retained SNP set and did not rediscover alternative SNPs from the original alignments.
